CUZZOio

Privacy notice · last updated October 29, 2026

The short version: Cuzzo io has no accounts, no tracking and no ads of its own, and it doesn't keep any information about you. When you play online, other players see your name, what you type in chat, and how your character moves. To connect you, their browsers can also see your IP address.

What other players see

  • Your name: what you typed in the name box, or your CrazyGames username if you're logged in to CrazyGames.
  • Your skin, your position and actions in the match, and your kills and deaths.
  • Chat messages you send. A filter hides swear words and slurs. Chat isn't saved anywhere; it disappears from the screen after a few seconds.
  • Your IP address. Online matches connect players' browsers directly to each other (peer-to-peer), and that kind of connection lets the other players' devices see your IP address. It's needed for the connection to work.

Our server

The game uses a small server (run on Cloudflare) for two things:

  • The Rooms list. When you host a room, the server keeps its name (your name + "'s room"), game mode, map, player count, whether a match is running, and whether it's public or private. A private room's code is never shown to other players. A room is deleted as soon as the host leaves, or within 75 seconds if they just close the game.
  • The relay. If two players can't connect directly (common on phone data and school networks), their match traffic is passed through Cloudflare's relay service instead.

The server sees your IP address while it answers you, only to stop spam (for example, at most 3 rooms per network). We don't save or log it. Cloudflare runs the computers this happens on, and it handles network data under its own privacy policy.

Matchmaking and game files

  • To find each other, players' browsers briefly exchange connection details through public matchmaking relays (open-source services that pass short messages between browsers). These include the room code and the network details needed to connect.
  • The game loads its multiplayer library from a public file server (jsDelivr). Like any website you visit, it sees your IP address when your browser downloads the file. Everything else, fonts included, comes with the game.

Saved on your device

Your name, your outfit, your progress (level, XP, unlocked outfits, daily challenges, campaign stars and best times, and simple totals like eliminations and wins), your free-for-all class and your settings (controls, sensitivity, graphics quality, bots on or off, and your Public/Private choice for hosting) are saved in your own browser so they're still there next time. Clearing your browser data removes them. Bots are played by your own computer; nothing about them is sent anywhere except to the players in your match.

On CrazyGames

When you play on CrazyGames, CrazyGames shows the ads, handles logins and provides your username, under the CrazyGames privacy policy. The game reads your username and CrazyGames' mute and chat settings, and it also gives your saved progress (the same list as above) to CrazyGames' save service so it follows your CrazyGames account to other devices.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, passwords or emails.
  • No tracking, analytics or advertising cookies of our own.
  • We don't store, sell or share any information about you.

Age

Cuzzo io is made for players aged 13 and up.

Questions

If you have a question about this notice, open an issue on the game's GitHub page. If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
